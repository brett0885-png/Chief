## Rubric — bird droppings (Brett, Nov 2)
Reference: rubric-bird-droppings-reference.jpg (Brett-supplied example of what droppings look like; he did NOT identify which of his videos contains the target — the hunt stays blind as to source/timestamp). Signature from the reference: a grayish, smeared, elongated streak ON the shingle field, low contrast against gray architectural shingles, running down-slope — NOT bright white splatter. Easy to mistake for a shingle patch or flashing shadow at drone distance. Search for the smear, not the splatter.
